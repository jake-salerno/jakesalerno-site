The End of Blind Faith

Keynote · 2026-02-16 · Denver, Colorado

Watch talk ↗Read organizer recap ↗

About the appearance

Jake Salerno delivered “The End of Blind Faith” at The Scaling Summit: House of AI during ETHDenver 2026. The 0G event recap identifies his keynote and its central principle: trust in AI should be supported by verification.

The recap describes a presentation connecting AI infrastructure with verifiability, data availability, and the ownership of AI agents. Jake introduced ERC-7857 as a framework for agents to be owned, verified, and transferred as onchain assets.

Discussion themes

  • Verification as a practical requirement for AI infrastructure.
  • How compute, storage, and data availability support AI applications.
  • Ownership and verification of agents through the ERC-7857 framework.

Summary based on 0G’s ETHDenver recap. The event took place February 16, 2026; the linked recording post was published February 26.
