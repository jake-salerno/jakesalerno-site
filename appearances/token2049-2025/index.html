About the appearance

Jake Salerno and 0G co-founder and CTO Ming Wu explored the relationship between AI and blockchain in a joint keynote at TOKEN2049 Singapore. Jake guided the conversation from 0G’s vision to its architecture, adoption, and plans for builders.

The discussion connects more accessible blockchain experiences with the need for transparent AI. Ming explains an approach that keeps resource-intensive work on decentralized compute and storage networks while recording concise proofs onchain. Jake brings the conversation back to usability, integrations, and the needs of developers across ecosystems.
