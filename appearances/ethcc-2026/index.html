Why Verification Should Be a 1st Class Citizen in AI

Talk / keynote · 2026-04-01 · Cannes, France

Watch talk ↗View session archive ↗

About the appearance

At EthCC[9], Jake Salerno presented the case for treating verification as a core requirement of AI systems. The official session archive describes a talk about transparency, trust, and the risks of closed, centralized models.

The session considers how more accountable, open AI development can address the uncertainty created by black-box systems. Its emphasis is on the ability to verify AI, rather than relying only on confidence in a provider.

Discussion themes

  • Transparency and verifiability as foundations for trust in AI.
  • Risks associated with opaque models and closed systems.
  • Mechanisms for more accountable and open AI development.
