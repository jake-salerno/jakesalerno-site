About the appearance

Jake Salerno joined the Multichain Day panel “Agentic Finance: Autonomous Capital, Execution, and AI-Native Markets” in Singapore with Charles de Pue and Dean Tribble. The session considered what changes when AI agents can execute financial transactions rather than only suggest them.

Jake discussed private, verifiable AI infrastructure, human approval of transactions, and the tradeoffs between verification, speed, and cost. The conversation also examined permission boundaries, execution safeguards, and the role of memory and context in agent workflows.

Discussion themes

  • 6:35–10:52: Jake discusses verifiable execution, privacy, attack surfaces, and keeping a human in the loop.
  • 13:34–15:10: matching the level of verification to the use case, with tradeoffs in speed, cost, and latency.
  • 24:49–26:51: Jake describes memory, context, and storage as infrastructure for agent workflows.
