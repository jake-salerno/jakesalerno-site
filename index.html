About

Bringing AI and blockchain to market

Jake Salerno leads commercial strategy, revenue growth, and ecosystem expansion at 0G Labs across business and consumer markets. Since joining in mid-2024, he has built and led its business development team and helped scale the ecosystem to more than 400 integrations.

His work spans private AI compute, encrypted storage, AI-native blockchain, and verifiable agent identity. He manages key infrastructure partnerships and works across product, engineering, marketing, and legal to translate market demand into product priorities, accelerate adoption, and create new revenue opportunities.

Previously, Jake led go-to-market for data products at Chainlink Labs. He brings nearly a decade of experience in crypto and a founder’s perspective, having built and exited multiple startups.

Based in Austin, Texas, Jake is driven by the belief that AI should be a public good. His work focuses on making it accessible and empowering people and organizations to build, own, and benefit from it.
